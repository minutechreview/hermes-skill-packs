# Social Calendar

## When to Use
- "Plan my posts for next month", "I don't know what to post for the shop", "festival posts".

## Procedure
1. Load the business facts from memory or `BUSINESS.md`: what they sell, best sellers, current offers, location, audience.
2. List the dates that matter that month: local holidays and festivals (for example Avurudu, Vesak, Ramadan, Diwali, Christmas), paydays, school terms, the season.
3. Plan 3-4 posts per week using a mix:
   - Product or service spotlight
   - Behind the scenes (making, packing, the team)
   - Customer story or review
   - Offer or event
   - Useful tip related to what they sell
4. For each post give: date, format (photo, carousel, reel, story), a photo/video idea the owner can shoot on a phone, and a ready caption.
5. Output as a simple table they can paste into a sheet.

## Pitfalls
- Check holiday dates for the right year; many move every year.
- Don't invent offers. Leave "[offer?]" where the owner must decide.
- Be respectful with religious holidays: greetings, not hard selling.

## Verification
- Every week has at least 3 posts and at least 2 different formats.
- Holiday dates are checked for the correct year.
