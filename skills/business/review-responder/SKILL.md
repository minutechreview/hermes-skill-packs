# Review Responder

## When to Use
- "Reply to these reviews", a pasted review, "what are customers complaining about?".

## Procedure
1. For each review, note: stars, what they liked, what went wrong, and anything specific (a staff name, a dish, a product).
2. **4-5 stars**: thank them by name, mention the specific thing they liked, invite them back. 2-3 lines. No copy-paste replies; each one should be different.
3. **1-3 stars**:
   - Thank them for telling you.
   - Acknowledge the specific problem without arguing.
   - Say what you're doing about it, only if the owner confirmed it.
   - Move it offline: "Please message us on <number/email> so we can make it right."
4. If the user gives you 10+ reviews, add a pattern summary: the top 3 praises, the top 3 complaints, and one suggested fix.

## Pitfalls
- Never reveal private details (order numbers, addresses, health info) in a public reply.
- Don't offer refunds or freebies unless the owner says so.
- Don't reply to reviews that look fake or abusive; flag them for the owner to report.

## Verification
- No two replies start the same way.
- Every negative reply moves the conversation offline.
